Basic Proportionality Theorem

Also called Thales' Theorem. Drag the point D along side AB and watch line DE stay parallel to BC — the two ratios it creates always stay equal.

If DE ∥ BC, then AD⁄DB = AE⁄EC

WHY IT WORKS

  • 1Point D divides AB in some ratio t. We place E on AC at the same ratio t from A.
  • 2Because AD⁄AB equals AE⁄AC, triangles ADE and ABC are similar (SAS similarity).
  • 3Similar triangles force corresponding sides DE and BC to be parallel — and AD⁄DB = AE⁄EC for every position of D.
